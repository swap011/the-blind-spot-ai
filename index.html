Identify What You Overlooked Before You Commit

People often decide based only on what is most visible. The Blind Spot unpacks your unstated assumptions, reveals cognitive biases, and poses calibrated Socratic questions without deciding for you.

🛡️
Strict Non-Prescriptive Mandate: This assistant never tells you what to choose or makes the decision for you. Its sole purpose is to widen your lens, stress-test reasoning, and help you think critically.
