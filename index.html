What Are You Missing Before You Decide?

People decide based on what is most visible — the stipend, the brand, the convenience. The Blind Spot surfaces the unstated assumptions and cognitive biases lurking beneath the surface, without telling you what to do.

🛡️
Non-Prescriptive Mandate: This assistant never makes the decision for you. It surfaces blind spots, poses calibrated questions, and widens your analytical lens — the choice remains entirely yours.
